HOW RENDER WORK RUNS

Brief to 4K master in about three weeks

STEP 1 Brief & references WEEK 1

Plans, drawings, and style references in — camera list and shot count agreed.
STEP 2 Blockout & cameras WEEK 1–2

Massing and angles approved in greyscale — geometry locked before detail work begins.
STEP 3 Materials & lighting WEEK 2–3

The photoreal pass — materials, landscape, and light tuned per style until it reads like a photograph.
STEP 4 4K delivery WEEK 3

4K masters plus the crops your media plan needs — and the source scene stays on file for the next campaign.

3D FAQ

Before you brief us

Still have questions? Talk to a human →

What styles can you model?
−

Any — for Villate we built Modern, Classic, Contemporary, Spanish, and Mexican as complete worlds. New styles are developed from your references in the first week.

What do we receive at the end?
+

4K master renders for every agreed camera, plus the crops your media plan needs. The source scene stays archived for future shots.

What do you need from us to start?
+

Plans or CAD if you have them; sketches and reference photos if you don't. We rebuild geometry from whatever exists — many projects start from a floor plan and a moodboard.

How do revisions work?
+

You approve at each gate — angles in greyscale, then materials and light. Because geometry is locked early, revision rounds turn in about 48 hours.

Can the renders become a real build?
+

Yes — that's how our exhibition work runs. The approved render becomes the build spec, and our events team builds it on site, as with EADS and HBL.
