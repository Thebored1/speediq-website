PRINT FAQ

Before you send files

Still have questions? Talk to a human →

Will the print match what I approved?
+

Yes — that's what the contract proof is for. We color-manage from proof to press and run press checks against it, so the sheet matches the proof you signed.

Digital or offset — which do I need?
+

We pick for you based on quantity and finish. Short runs and fast turnarounds go digital; long runs and special colors go offset — quoted both ways when it's close.

Do you design the artwork too?
+

We can — the same studio behind our identity and packaging work designs to your guidelines. Or send print-ready files and we'll preflight them before press.

What's the largest run you handle?
+

Runs up to 30,000 units on our offset presses, with finishing and die-cutting in the same building — so a large run never leaves our quality control.

Can you deliver straight to our stores?
+

Yes — production management covers kitting and distribution. We can ship to one dock or split a run across branches, packed and labelled per location.
