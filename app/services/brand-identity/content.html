IDENTITY FAQ

Before you brief us

Still have questions? Talk to a human →

How long does an identity take?
−

3–4 weeks for a complete identity. Add a week or two when naming or a large collateral set — packaging ranges, annual reports — is in scope.

What do we receive at the end?
+

Final logo files in every format, the full guidelines document, source files, and templates for everything in scope. You own all of it.

Can you refresh our identity instead of starting over?
+

Yes — evolution over transformation is how we work. We keep the equity you've built and rebuild the system around it.

Do you also produce the printed collateral?
+

Yes. Print & production is in-house, so cards, reports, packaging, and signage ship from the same team that designed them — no handoff, no drift.

How does pricing work for identity projects?
+

Fixed scope, quoted up front after a free audit — no hourly meters. Collateral and production runs are quoted per piece.
