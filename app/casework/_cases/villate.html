CASEWORK — 04 / 06 · IDENTITY + 3D + CAMPAIGN

Villate — five villas sold from a screen.

Five villa styles, none built yet — one mark, a concept color per style, and photoreal 3D that let buyers approve homes before ground was broken.

SECTOR
Real estate
SCOPE
Identity · 3D · Campaign
STYLES
Five, one family
THE CHALLENGE

Sell homes that don't exist yet.

Villate was launching five villa styles — Modern, Classic, Contemporary, Spanish, Mexican — with nothing built. Buyers had to fall for finished homes from a brochure and a screen, and five distinct styles had to still read as one development.

THE APPROACH
01
One mark, five style brands

Logo and slogan for the development, then a sub-brand per villa style — each with its own concept color, all inside one family.

02
Photoreal 3D, exterior and interior

Every style modeled as one continuous world — facades, landscape, lighting, and furnished interiors — rendered at campaign resolution.

03
The campaign, from the renders

Brochures, hoardings, and sales material built directly on the 3D imagery — the world buyers saw was the world being sold.
